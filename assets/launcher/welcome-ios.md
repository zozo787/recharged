# Welcome to Recharged

Recharged plays **Mario Strikers Charged** natively on your iPhone and iPad, from your own copy of the game. This launcher is where you set it up and start it; the pages on the left hold everything.

## Get playing

1. **Add your game.** On the **Game** page, tap **Choose file...** and pick your Mario Strikers Charged image (ISO, RVZ or WBFS) wherever it is: Files, iCloud Drive or a USB drive. It is copied into Recharged's own folder. You can also put the image yourself in the Files app, under On My iPhone (or iPad) > Recharged > game; the launcher finds it there.
2. **Pick your controls.** On **Controls**, player 1 plays with the touch screen. Tap the sliders button beside it to move the on-screen controls where your thumbs fall, size them, and set their opacity and the tap on each press. A Bluetooth gamepad (Xbox, PlayStation and others) plays instead as soon as it is connected; with none connected, the touch screen plays.
3. **Press Play.** The **Play** page starts the game with your settings. To leave the game, tap the small cross at the top left and confirm: the app closes, and you open it again to come back to this launcher.

## Make it yours

- **Display** and **Audio**: the picture, the frame rate and the sound levels.
- **Miis**: create your Mii here.
- **Sidekicks** and **Match rules**: the sidekicks each captain starts with, and presets of the match settings and cheats.
- **Controls**: the game's rumble, on gamepads and on the iPhone itself, and its strength.
- **Gameplay**: optional tweaks to the original game, and HD texture packs.

## Good to know

- Changes are kept when you tap **Save** at the bottom right; **Revert** brings back the saved settings.
- Each setting says what it does under its name. A gamepad works in the launcher too: the D-pad moves, A chooses, B goes back.
- Your settings, saves and logs are in the Files app, in the Recharged folder.
- Recharged does not update itself on iOS: to get a newer version, install its new .ipa the way you installed this one.
- Something wrong? **About** has your build's details and the logs for a bug report, and the community on Discord.

You can open this guide again with the light bulb at the bottom, beside **Revert**.
